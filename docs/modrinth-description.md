## 先说这是什么

**中文名：幸运四叶草。**

Minecraft 里"努力就有回报"的部分已经够多了，这个模组补的是另一半：**运气**。

打草、砍树、挖矿、翻结构箱子，都有机会摸到一棵四叶草。右键撕开，掉点东西。可能是一把泥土，也可能是鞘翅。

就这么点事。我一开始只想做一棵草，后来想着"那不如加点等级"，再后来"那不如加点意外"，最后就变成现在这样了。

## 草从哪来

- **打草**（短草、高草、蕨、大蕨）：8%
- **结构箱子**：35%
- **挖矿**：2%，钻石/绿宝石/远古残骸这类 1.5%
- **砍自然生成的木头**：0.8%

挖矿掉草是**看矿石给草**：煤矿掉煤炭草，铁矿掉铁草，远古残骸掉下界合金草。所以挖矿这件事，第一次有了"随手一镐会不会有惊喜"的味道。

砍树那个 0.8% 确实低，但原木产量太大了，我不想让砍树变成刷草的活儿。

有个限制得提前说清楚：**只有自然生成的方块才掉草**。自己搭的刷石机、自己摆下去的原木，撕不出任何东西。不然在服务器里搭条生产线，三分钟就能把概率玩坏。

## 十种草

- **普通**：1 份。泥土、圆石、木板、火把、种子、燧石
- **木头**：1 份。木板木棍、木炭、熔炉、玻璃、胡萝卜土豆
- **煤炭**：1 份。铁锭铜锭、灯笼、桶、剪刀、皮革套、蛋糕
- **铜**：2 份。铁锭、铁轨矿车、铁剑铁镐、铁套、指南针
- **青金石**：2 份。青金石、书架、金苹果、末影珍珠、望远镜
- **铁**：2 份。铁锭金锭、金苹果、金胡萝卜、附魔台、信标、三叉戟
- **黄金**：2 份。金套金剑、钻石、烈焰棒、恶魂之泪、末影之眼
- **钻石**：3 份。钻石套、钻石块、鞘翅、潜影盒、不死图腾、海洋之心
- **绿宝石**：3 份。绿宝石块、钻石块、龙蛋、末影水晶、经验瓶、潮涌核心
- **下界合金**：3 份。下界合金锭/剑/镐/全套甲、下界之星、龙蛋、鞘翅

**钻石及以上是认真的。** 3 份意味着一次能摸出三件，运气好一镐下去直接毕业。这也是为什么它们的掉率压得那么死。

等级是靠**物品本身的材质**区分的（木头草、铁草、下界合金草），不是靠名字前缀。工具提示里有星级，一眼能看出这棵值不值得留。

## 另外两种草

**模组幸运草**——从你装的所有其它模组里随机抽一件东西。装了 TACZ 的话，大概率直接给你枪械、配件、弹药这类能用的成品，而不是那些工作台和标靶。抽到什么全看你装了什么，这棵草本质上是"给整合包开盲盒"。

**生物幸运草**——右键随机召唤一只原版生物，从牛、羊、猫、狐狸到铁傀儡，也有一小撮是僵尸骷髅苦力怕。纯属好玩，别在自家客厅开。

还有一种 **随机幸运草包裹**，撕开随机给一棵草。适合送给朋友，看对方开出什么。

## 事件：18 种，其中两个要你动手

开草时有 2% 概率触发一场"幸运事件"，屏幕中央弹出大字，天降一波奖励。矿石雨、钻石雨、附魔书雨（真书假书混着掉，会告诉你几本是真的）、金苹果雨、烟花秀、时空跳跃、流浪商人、铁傀儡保镖、蜂群、乐音……

这些是纯送的。另外有两个得你自己动：

- **寻宝竞速**——6 份奖励撒在四周 8 到 18 格的地方，跑过去捡。每份都带白色发光轮廓和头顶光柱，隔着树和坡也能看见。
- **命运抉择**——你身边会立起两个一模一样的箱子，中间一块告示牌写着「稳妥，还是赌博？」。开一个（或者砸一个），另一个立刻塌缩，强制二选一。稳的是钻石 ×6；赌的一半概率 3 块下界合金，一半概率 4 块腐肉。两个箱子都是空的，靠信息模组偷看不了内容。

## 药水：把 2% 拉到 50%

平时 2% 太低，专门做了两档药水：

- **幸运药剂**：糖 + 金粒 + 普通草，事件概率 20%，持续 60 秒
- **事件药水**：烈焰粉 + 金粒 + 普通草，事件概率 50%，持续 120 秒

高级那瓶带一层附魔光晕（纯装饰，没有实际附魔）。喝下去猛撕草，两分钟能看三四场事件。

## 成就：17 个

打开进度界面，会多出一个「幸运四叶草」标签页。分三条线：十级阶梯（每一种草一个）、特殊收集（模组草 / 生物草 / 包裹 / 两瓶药水）、以及两个挑战——「合金岁月」（拿到下界合金草）和「十全十美」（十种全部拥有过，200 经验）。

成就是靠「拥有物品」触发的，不需要额外操作。所以创造模式直接拿也能解锁，它证明不了你是挖出来的，纯粹给收集控一个目标。

## JEI 掉落图鉴

装了 JEI 的话，在 JEI 里点任意一棵幸运草（默认 `U` 键），会多出一页「幸运草可能开出什么」：左边是草本身，右边列出这一档全部可能掉落，悬停看名字。每档独立一页，底部写明每项概率（等概率 1/N）和这份草一共抽几次。

页面直接读取模组的真实掉落池，改了掉落表会自动跟着变，不会出现"说明和实际不一致"。

## 幸运口袋

默认按 <kbd>P</kbd> 打开（键位可以改）。

一页 54 格，左右翻页，上面有搜索框——打"钻石"两个字，剩下的格子里就只剩钻石相关的东西。找东西比翻箱子快。

它还有个更实用的地方：**草开出东西而背包满了的时候，掉落物会自动进袋子**，不会掉在地上被岩浆吃掉。挖矿挖到一半背包爆仓，也不用回头捡。

## 想调数值

装完会在 `config/magicclover.json` 生成配置，记事本就能改。常用的几项：

- `grassDropChance`：打草掉草概率，默认 0.08
- `structureCloverChance`：结构箱子出草概率，默认 0.35
- `eventChance`：平时触发事件概率，默认 0.02
- `eventChanceBuffed`：喝事件药水后的概率，默认 0.5
- `buffDuration`：事件药水持续秒数，默认 120
- `lesserEventChance`：喝幸运药剂后的概率，默认 0.2
- `lesserBuffDuration`：幸运药剂持续秒数，默认 60
- `eventItemMarker`：参与型事件的发光轮廓与光柱，默认 true
- `modCloverTaczChance`：模组草开出 TACZ 成品的概率，默认 0.9
- `modItemBlacklist`：不想被模组草抽到的物品 id，默认空

想让服务器变成"草随便掉"，把前两项改成 1.0 就行。

## 装法与兼容

- Minecraft **1.21.10 / 1.21.11**（同一个 jar 通用），Fabric Loader 0.20.1+，Java 21
- 需要 **Fabric API**
- **客户端和服务端都要装**（掉落判定、事件、口袋数据都在服务端）
- 物品名内置简中和英文，游戏语言切了会跟着变
- 没做 Forge 版本，也不打算做

## 几个我已经知道的坑

**旧存档的箱子不会补草。** 结构箱子的内容在区块生成那一刻就算好了，模组后装上去只对没生成过的区块生效。想在新存档里立刻看到效果，就开个新世界。

**事件是聊天栏 + 屏幕大字，没有 GUI。** 想让它更显眼，去配置把 `eventTitle` 关掉反而更安静——这个开关是给"不想被大字糊脸"的人准备的。

**模组草的池子依赖你装了什么。** 只装原版的话它抽不出东西，会退回普通草的行为。装一百个模组的话，抽到家具的概率比抽到武器高，这个我没法控制。

**口袋里没有"整理"按钮。** 搜索能用，排序没做。以后可能会加。

## 最后

这模组没有任务、没有每日、没有要你肝的东西。它就是在你打草、挖矿、翻箱子的路上，偶尔扔给你一点意外。

真要说目标也有——17 个成就摆在那儿，收集控可以慢慢点亮；不点亮也不影响你玩。

运气好的时候别太得意，运气差的时候……反正泥土也能垫脚。

## 来路

玩法灵感来自 konwboj 的经典模组《幸运四叶草 / Magic Clover》（Forge，1.6.2 ~ 1.12.2）。那个版本停更多年，原帖也已经不好找了——这个模组是我为 1.21.10 / 1.21.11 重写的一版，代码全部重写，贴图全部重绘，没有使用原版的任何资源文件。

说是"重制"，其实更接近"拿着同一个想法，按我自己的口味做了十倍的量"。如果原作者哪天回来，这个页面随时愿意配合他的任何要求。

## English

*(English translation of the description above. Item names are localised in both Chinese and English.)*

**This mod is about luck.**

Chop grass, fell trees, mine ore, loot structure chests — each of them has a small chance to drop a four-leaf clover. Right-click it and it tears open into loot. Sometimes it's dirt. Sometimes it's an elytra.

It started as one clover. Then it needed tiers. Then it needed "what if something unexpected happened". This is where that ended up.

### Where clovers come from

- **Grass and ferns**: 8%
- **Structure chests**: 35%
- **Mining**: 2%, or 1.5% for diamond, emerald and ancient debris
- **Logs**: 0.8%

Mining drops follow the ore: coal ore gives a coal clover, iron ore an iron clover, ancient debris a netherite clover.

Only **naturally generated blocks** drop clovers. Anything you placed yourself won't. Otherwise a cobblestone generator turns the whole thing into a factory in ten minutes, and the 0.8% on logs is low for the same reason — trees are too easy to farm.

### Ten tiers

- **Basic** — 1 roll: dirt, cobblestone, planks, torches, seeds, flint
- **Wooden** — 1 roll: planks, sticks, charcoal, furnace, glass, carrots, potatoes
- **Coal** — 1 roll: iron and copper ingots, lanterns, buckets, shears, leather armour
- **Copper** — 2 rolls: iron ingots, rails, minecarts, iron sword and pickaxe, iron armour
- **Lapis** — 2 rolls: lapis, bookshelves, golden apples, ender pearls, spyglass
- **Iron** — 2 rolls: iron and gold ingots, enchanting table, beacon, trident
- **Golden** — 2 rolls: gold armour, diamonds, blaze rods, ghast tears, eyes of ender
- **Diamond** — 3 rolls: diamond armour, diamond blocks, elytra, shulker box, totem
- **Emerald** — 3 rolls: emerald blocks, diamond blocks, dragon egg, end crystal, conduits
- **Netherite** — 3 rolls: netherite ingots, sword, pickaxe, full armour, nether star, elytra

**Diamond and above are the real thing.** Three rolls means a single clover can hand you three items at once, which is why the drop rates are kept that tight.

### Two odd ones

**Mod Clover** draws a random item from every other mod you have installed. With TACZ present it leans toward actual guns, attachments and ammo rather than workbenches and targets.

**Mob Clover** spawns a random vanilla mob — mostly cows, cats, foxes and goats, with a few zombies and creepers mixed in. Don't open it in your living room.

**Random Clover Bundle** gives you a clover of random tier. Good for handing to a friend and watching what they get.

### Events

Every clover has a 2% chance to fire one of **18 events**: an on-screen title, a particle burst, and loot falling from the sky. Ore rain, diamond rain, enchanted book rain (real books and duds mixed together, and it tells you how many were real), golden apples, fireworks, time skips, wandering traders, iron golems...

Two of them want you to do something:

- **Treasure Rush** — six rewards scattered 8 to 18 blocks away, each with a glowing outline and a beam of light so you can spot them behind trees and hills.
- **Fate's Choice** — two identical chests appear beside you, with a sign between them asking "safe, or gamble?". Open one (or break one) and the other collapses instantly. Safe is 6 diamonds; gamble is a coin flip between 3 netherite ingots and 4 rotten flesh. Both chests are empty until opened, so HUD mods can't spoil it.

### Potions

- **Lucky Tonic** — sugar + gold nugget + clover, 20% event chance for 60 seconds
- **Event Potion** — blaze powder + gold nugget + clover, 50% event chance for 120 seconds

The stronger one carries an enchantment glint. Purely cosmetic.

### Achievements

There are 17 of them, in their own advancement tab: one per clover tier, one each for the special clovers and potions, plus two challenges — *Alloy Era* (get a netherite clover) and *Perfect Ten* (own all ten tiers at some point).

They trigger on "having the item", so creative mode unlocks them too. They're a collection goal, not a proof you mined anything.

### JEI loot book

With JEI installed, press `U` on any clover to open a new page: the clover on the left, its entire loot pool on the right. One page per tier, with the exact odds (equal 1/N) and how many rolls that tier gets.

The page reads the real loot pool straight from the mod, so it never drifts out of sync.

### Lucky Pouch

Press <kbd>P</kbd> (rebindable). 54 slots per page, paging, and a search box — type "diamond" and only diamond-related slots stay visible. Anything that would overflow your inventory goes in here instead of onto the ground, which matters when one clover hands you three items.

### Config

Everything lives in `config/magicclover.json`: drop rates, event chance, buff durations, plus `eventItemMarker` if you'd rather not have the glowing markers on event loot.

### Requirements

Minecraft 1.21.10 / 1.21.11 · Fabric Loader 0.20.1+ · Fabric API · Java 21. Install on **both** client and server — drops, events and pouch data are all server-side. No Forge or NeoForge version, and I don't plan to make one.

### Things I already know are imperfect

- Chests in already-generated chunks won't retroactively contain clovers. New world, or new chunks.
- Events are chat messages plus a screen title. There's no GUI.
- The Mod Clover pool depends entirely on what you have installed. With no other mods it falls back to basic behaviour; with a hundred, you'll see furniture more often than weapons.
- The pouch has search but no sort button. Maybe later.

### Where this came from

The idea comes from konwboj's classic mod *Magic Clover* (Forge, 1.6.2 – 1.12.2), which has been abandoned for years and whose original thread is hard to find these days. This is a fresh implementation for 1.21.10 / 1.21.11: the code was written from scratch and every texture was redrawn — no assets from the original are used.

"Remake" is a strong word for it. It's closer to taking the same idea and doing ten times as much with it. If the original author ever comes back, this page will cooperate with whatever they'd like.


## 1.6.2 补充说明

随机幸运草包裹新增一条简易合成：**4 小麦 + 2 普通草 → 1 包裹**（无序），和原来的 3 皮革 + 6 普通草并存。没养牛的玩家用小麦把草捆起来就行。

**English:** The random clover bundle now has a second recipe: **4 wheat + 2 basic clovers → 1 bundle** (shapeless), alongside the original leather recipe. No cow farm required.


## 1.6.4 补充说明

基础幸运草新增合成路线：**4 个相同的种子 → 1 棵基础幸运草**（小麦 / 西瓜 / 南瓜 / 甜菜 / 火把花种子均可）。

**English:** Basic clovers can now be crafted from **4 identical seeds** (wheat / melon / pumpkin / beetroot / torchflower).

**English:** Enchanted books from clovers now always carry one random enchantment. The book-rain event's real books are capped at one enchantment too.


## 1.6.6 补充说明

奖池重平衡：按稀有度加权抽取（不是等概率）。传说物品从"每档约 14%"降到单件约 2%；每档新增 2~3 件独占物品，清掉高档里的盾牌等填充物。JEI 图鉴里的说明文字也同步更新。

**English:** Loot pools are now weighted by rarity instead of uniform. Legendary items dropped from ~14% to ~2% each. Each tier gained exclusive items, and filler like shields was removed from high tiers.


## 1.6.7 补充说明

奖池再次扩充：总物品种类 116 → 136，移除龙蛋，削减跨档重复，并把低价值可堆叠物品改为成组掉落（2~16 个）。传说物品单件概率仍约 2%。

**English:** Loot pools expanded to 136 items. Dragon eggs removed (one per world is a trophy, not loot), cross-tier duplicates reduced, and low-value items now drop in stacks of 2-16 instead of a single lonely item.


## 1.7.0 枯叶幸运草

新增一种从**落叶堆**小概率掉落的赌博草：**枯叶幸运草**。右键开启时约 75% 倒霉、20% 小赚、5% 血赚。倒霉包括扣血、30~60 秒负面 buff、随机掉一件背包物品到地上——惩罚都有下限，不会一击必杀，也不会销毁物品。概率可在配置里调整。

**English:** The Withered Clover is a gamble item dropped by leaf litter. Right-click: ~75% bad luck (damage capped at 1 heart remaining, timed debuffs, or an item dropped on the ground), 20% small win, 5% jackpot. Odds are configurable.
